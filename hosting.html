Operations

Maintenance that happens before you notice a problem

Most lost revenue in mining is not caused by price — it is caused by machines quietly sitting idle. Our operations routine is designed to catch that within hours, not weeks.

  • Daily walkthroughs. Physical inspection of every aisle with thermal spot checks and dust assessment.
  • Alert-driven response. Offline or underperforming units are triaged the same working day.
  • Free first-line repairs. Reboots, reseats, cable and fan work are included; component parts are quoted at cost.
  • Scheduled deep cleaning. Periodic dust extraction and filter replacement to protect hashboards in a desert climate.
