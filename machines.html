How we sell

No mystery boxes, no vague “working condition” claims

Every machine we sell is inspected in our own workshop before it reaches you. You receive the real numbers, not a marketing sheet.

  • 72-hour bench test. Hashrate, board health, error rate and temperature logged before sale.
  • Written condition report. Serial number, test results and real photos of the exact unit you buy.
  • Warranty in writing. 12 months on new hardware; 30–90 day workshop warranty on tested used units.
  • UAE import handled. Customs clearance, duties and local delivery arranged by us.
  • Host-on-arrival option. Choose hosting at checkout and your machines never leave our care.
